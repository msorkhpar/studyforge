⛔ **Confirmed rather than merely accepted, and the coordinator's reasoning was
right on both halves.** ⚠️ **My own rule says a `Team` task is scheduled by the

commit there is no seam to split on, and splitting earlier is the collision-pair
rule's exact failure.**

## Findings

- `[structural]` **`PO-20/1`** — ⛔ **A park point records an obligation and not a
  result.** ⚠️ **Two rows read *"must be `done` or parked before `SF-12`"* and both
  were discharged by never starting, which is legitimate and is indistinguishable
  from work in flight.** ⭐ **Remedied on both rows here — a filler now records
  `done` or `PARKED @ <ref>`** — ⛔ **and the general form is the ref rule again: an
  obligation is not a measurement.**
- `[structural]` **`PO-20/2`** — ⛔ **Scheduling order tracks MINTING order, not
  priority.** ⚠️ **`W28` and `W29` were minted after `FND-08`/`FND-09` and
  scheduled ahead of them; `FND-09` has waited two waves behind three newer
  rows.** ⭐ **A newly minted row arrives with its argument fresh in the writer's
  head, which is exactly why it wins a slot it was not ranked into.** ⛔ **Not
  fixed: the remedy is a ranked queue per developer, which is a change to how the
  board is written and wants a wave of its own.**
- `[local]` **`PO-20/3`** — ⛔ **Check 4 did not go stale under me, and the cause is
  the moment rather than the instrument.** ⚠️ **Three rounds recorded mid-run
  staleness as a property of the close run; this run had no branch awaiting a
  verdict and drifted nowhere.** ⭐ **So the scheduling rule is: run check 4 when
  the review queue is empty**, which is also when `git log` is a complete
  enumeration of what moved.
- `[structural]` **`PO-20/4`** — ⛔ **Two catalogue contributions had already been
  adopted invisibly.** ⚠️ **One was absorbed into entry 5's closing paragraph and
  one *is* entry 8's correction banner — neither contributor could have known, and
  neither is listed anywhere as counterparted.** ⭐ **Recorded as *already carried*
  in the refusals table**, ⛔ **because adoption-without-a-record is the same
  observable as neglect, which is `F23` seen from the other side.**
- `[local]` **`PO-20/5`** — ⛔ **The round-4 note attributes the stranding to
  permissions** (*"the catalogue lives in a repository this role may not write
  to"*). ⚠️ **The catalogue's own header has always invited entries from either
  side.** ⭐ **The wall was that adoption had no owner** — relayed, because a
  contributor who believes the wall is permission will stop contributing rather
  than escalate.
- `[structural]` **`PO-20/6`** — ⛔ **`SF-12` is a `Team`-sized task with one
  author, and that is the slowest safe arrangement.** ⚠️ **If it slips, the wave
  slips with it, and the mitigation (the named split point) only works if the
  first half lands early.** ⭐ **So the signal is a commit, not a date: a `SF-12`
  that has not committed `page/blocks/` by mid-wave is the moment to re-dispatch.**
