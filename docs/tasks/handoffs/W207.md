## Decisions

⛔ **`max_files` IS THE LIMIT. Deleting the key from the epic is REFUSED.**

⭐ **The row's own test decided it** — *"whether a corpus can cross it while
under both byte limits and be right to stop"*. It can, and narration is the
shape that does: 20 000 clips at 20 KB each is 400 MB, under a 2 GiB total and a
five-hundredth of a 100 MiB per-file block, so **both byte ceilings say yes**
while the clone is a repository every `clone`, `status` and `checkout` pays
20 000 paths for. ⚠️ A repository is paid for per path as well as per byte, and
no byte limit can state that.

⛔ **It has NO DEFAULT, and that is the second half of the decision.** The two
byte defaults trace to §5's measured numbers; nobody measured a count, and a
number invented here would refuse builds on a ceiling no round chose. ⭐
**Unstated is unbounded** — the count is measured and reported either way.

### ⛔ Compatibility: what is ASSERTED and what is NOT

⭐ **BACKWARD — asserted, and in the suite.** A manifest with no `media` key, and
one whose `media` block omits `max_files`, parse identically at `corpus_api`
**1, 2 and 3**, yielding `max_files is None` and equal to `DEFAULT_MEDIA`.
⛔ Nothing that parsed before parses differently.

⭐ **FORWARD — asserted, and measured against the previous build's OWN CODE
rather than by reading it.** `git archive 6ffba1e src/studyforge` into the
scratchpad, that tree on `PYTHONPATH`; host, this checkout, role `wt/dev2`:

| fed to the reader at `6ffba1e` | what it said |
|---|---|
| `corpus_api: 3` + `media.max_files` | ⭐ `ManifestError` — *"declares corpus_api 3; this build speaks corpus_api [1, 2]"* |
| `corpus_api: 2` + `media.max_files` | ⚠️ `ManifestError` — *"'media' has unknown key(s), ['max_files']"* |

⛔ **That table is the whole argument for the bump.** The first sentence sends an
integrator to upgrade the framework; the second **blames the corpus for the
framework's age**. ⭐ **The new gate makes the second row unproducible**: a
manifest declaring `max_files` under `1` or `2` is refused here, naming both
numbers.

⛔ **WHAT I DO NOT ASSERT.** ⚠️ **There is no partial reading and no
negotiation** — an older build refuses the *whole* manifest, not the key alone.
That is R9 working, and a corpus author who wants one file readable by two
framework ages has no mechanism; I did not build one. ⚠️ **I assert nothing
about any corpus repository on disk**: `workspace.json` pins components, and
nothing pins a framework version against a corpus.
