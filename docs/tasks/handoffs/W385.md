## What landed

- `ServedCorpus(corpus, root, relative, startup, digest, scan_root=None)`. Given `None`,
  `scan_root` becomes `root`, so the field is always a path. `rescan` scans `scan_root`. The
  progress store (`progress()`) and the unit documents stay at `root`.
- `discover` leaves `scan_root` unset, so every corpus it finds is scanned where it sits.
  `site_discovery` passes the site, so a site `build --out` wrote elsewhere is scanned there.

## Decisions

- ⭐ **A field with a default, not a subclass.** `W380/2` said the distinction belongs on
  `ServedCorpus`. A keyword field defaulting to `root` keeps every existing positional
  constructor call valid, and no caller now needs to know that a subclass overrides a scan.
- ⭐ **Defaulted in `__post_init__`, not by a property over `None`.** A reader of `scan_root`
  always gets a `Path`, never a sentinel it must resolve itself.
