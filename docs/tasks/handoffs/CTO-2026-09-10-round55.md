## ⛔ Ruling 235 — the cause/class split is RIGHT; an UNREACHABLE DEADLINE does not cost a harness its AUTHORITY, it costs it the RIGHT TO QUOTE THE BOUND

⭐ **`W36/2` CONFIRMED by reading the code, and it is exactly as stated.**
`call()` sets `deadline = time.monotonic() + CALL_TIMEOUT` and loops
`while time.monotonic() < deadline`, but the loop body is `self._receive()`,
which blocks in `os.read` on a live pipe with no deadline. ⛔ **So
`BrowserError(f"{method}: no answer in {CALL_TIMEOUT:.0f}s")` is unreachable for
a browser that is UP AND SILENT; it fires only if the pipe CLOSES.**

> ⭐ **(a) The split is RATIFIED and it is the right call.** A row removes the
> CAUSE it owns and ROUTES the CLASS it does not. ⛔ `shm_size` is a fact about
> how this image is RUN and `compose.yaml` owns it; a deadlined read is a change
> to the harness's TRANSPORT and `QA-03` owns that. ⚠️ **And there is a second
> reason better than ownership: `select`-ing on the fd is a different I/O path,
> so fixing it here would have changed what every one of the 57 first readings
> was taken against, inside the row that was establishing them.**
>
> ⛔ **(b) The harness MAY be the authority, and the reasoning is the part that
> generalises: a hang WITHHOLDS a reading; it does not produce a false one.**
> ⭐ **Ruling 40 assigns authority to an ENVIRONMENT, not a warranty that a
> reading always arrives.** An instrument that sometimes returns nothing is
> weaker than one that always returns something, but it never certifies a wrong
> answer by this mechanism — and the alternative reading, that authority is
> forfeit, would strip the container of authority over **5349** passing
> assertions on account of one unexercised path.
>
> ⛔ **(c) What IS forfeit is the BOUND.** `CALL_TIMEOUT = 30.0` and the message
> *"no answer in 30s"* together DECLARE a thirty-second guarantee the code cannot
> enforce. ⚠️ **That is Ruling 208 applied to a NUMBER rather than to a
> population: an instrument that prints a reach it does not have is a false
> attestation.** ⭐ **So until the read is deadlined, no record, review, docstring
> or brief may cite `CALL_TIMEOUT` as a bound** — and a reviewer who meets a
> wedged run reports *no reading*, never *a thirty-second timeout*.
>
> ⭐ **(d) The CHEAP half is outside the harness and I am routing it separately,
> because it is worth far less than `W36/2` and costs almost nothing.** An outer
