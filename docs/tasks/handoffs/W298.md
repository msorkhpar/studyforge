## Decisions

- ⛔ **RULED: the single minter is `corpus.placement`, and the opposite direction is not a
  preference but an ImportError.** The row required the direction argued out loud and asked
  whether [`W199`](W199.md)'s reasoning still holds. ⭐ **It does not transfer as written — its
  ground was `validate` and the judge/writer argument — but the conclusion survives on a
  stronger measurement of its own:**
  - ⛔ **Measured, at `5fca2ea`, in a throwaway copy of `src/` outside every checkout:** patching
    `names.py` to take the segment from `studyforge.skills.adapter` and importing
    `studyforge.corpus.placement` raises `ImportError: cannot import name 'ARCHIVE_DIRNAME' from
    partially initialized module 'studyforge.corpus.placement' (most likely due to a circular
    import)`, at `layout.py`'s own import line. ⭐ The cycle is direct and needs no third
    package, because `skills.adapter.layout` already imports `corpus.placement`.
  - **Measured with `git grep`:** `corpus/` holds **no** import of `skills` at all, so the
    chosen direction adds **zero** edges — the one it needs already exists.
  - [`layout.py`](../../../src/studyforge/skills/adapter/layout.py)'s own contract already
    names `corpus.placement` as where its segments come from, and
    [`module-structure.md`](../../conventions/module-structure.md) makes a stated direction part
    of the package contract.
- ⛔ **RULED, and it is the premise the row did not state: the two constants name segments in
  TWO DIFFERENT TREES, and binding them is therefore a claim that had to be checked.**
  `UNITS_DIR` is the **archive**'s segment — `<archive>/<address>/units/unit-NN/`, written by an
  adapter, read by `validate.source.membership` and by a build. `UNITS_DIRNAME`'s only consumer
  is [`tree.py`](../../../src/studyforge/corpus/placement/tree.py), the **generated site**'s
  placer. ⚠️ **Nothing would break at run time if those two directories were spelled
  differently**, so "one value" is not self-evident and a taker who binds them without saying so
  has hidden a decision.
  - ⭐ **The ground for binding is a contract already in the tree, in `tree.py`'s own
    docstring:** the shape below a container is *"identical, segment for segment … so every href
    a page holds to its own media is unchanged"*. ⛔ **That sentence is exactly the kind of
    invariant that breaks with nothing failing** — which is the defect class [`W199`](W199.md)
    closed — and `generate/media.py` depends on both ends of it in one loop, asking
    `Layout.unit_files` where a unit's media IS and placement where it GOES.
  - ⛔ **So the binding is correct, and the residual risk is recorded rather than hidden:** the
    day a profile wants a site layout that diverges from the archive's, the remedy is a second
    NAME with its own contract, not a second literal — and the cross-tree pin is what will fail
    and say so.
- ⛔ **RULED (the instrument): a MINT is distinguished from a JSON KEY by USE, not by spelling,
  and the distinction is asserted in both directions.** The row allowed a stated reason the
  distinction is not needed; there is no such reason — measured, a spelled-once scan over
  `units` reds **six** modules that mint no directory. ⭐ The three non-mint shapes are named in
  the census so the next reader knows what was excluded and why.
  - ⚠️ **The instrument's limit is stated in its own docstring rather than discovered later:**
    it reads `pathlib` joins, which is what this framework uses. `os.path.join`, `"/".join` and
    an f-string-assembled path would not be seen.
- ⛔ **`UNITS_DIR` STAYS on `skills.adapter.__all__`, as a binding** — [`W199`](W199.md)'s rider
  applied unchanged, and this row's clause 3 is met the way that rider says: an adapter's whole
  vocabulary arrives through one package (R19), there is one definition, and the surviving name
  now has a CLOSED check rather than a spot assertion.

## Surprises

- ⛔ **The row's own framing of the hard part was right but incomplete.** It says `units` is
  *"mostly a JSON key"*. Measured, the six non-mint spellings are **three** shapes, and the
  third is the interesting one: `UNITS = "units/"` in two `serve/routes` modules is a **URL**
  prefix. ⚠️ A census that called them all keys would have been wrong in its own comment, and
  the URL pair is a finding in its own right (`W298/1`).
- ⭐ **The premise "two mints of one value" was true only after an argument.** Two different
  trees share a spelling; what makes them one value is a sentence in a docstring, not a shared
  directory. ⚠️ Had that sentence not existed, the honest outcome of this row would have been to
  REPORT clause 1 rather than meet it.
- ⛔ **`skills.adapter`'s existing surface test could not fail the way clause 3 needs.** It
  walks `__all__` to check `__all__`, so it agrees with itself. Measured under P3.
- ⚠️ **A scan parameterised by a constant cannot detect a change to that constant** (P2). Worth
  stating because it is easy to read the new test as a value guard, and it is not one.
