## Decisions

### 1. The population is the paragraph, and that is the whole discriminator

⛔ **A restated-as-delivered clause is told from a violation by WHERE IT SITS,
not by any marker.** The population is the `**Acceptance.**` marker line and the
unbroken lines under it. `SPLIT` (Ruling 151) and `RESTATED AS DELIVERED`
(Ruling 166) are different dispositions with **one mechanical consequence**: both
move the offending text out of that paragraph and into a struck quotation under a
`####` heading. ⭐ **So the sweep never has to tell a restatement from a split at
all** — neither is in its population, and both remain readable in the document.

`test_a_restated_clause_leaves_the_population_while_its_text_stays_in_the_document`
asserts **both halves** for all four members, because either half alone is
satisfiable by the wrong thing: text still promised, or text quietly deleted.

⚠️ **A marker-based predicate was considered and rejected.** It would have keyed
on the annotation's own wording, which the next author is free to spell
differently — and a sweep that trusts a heading is a sweep that a heading can
turn off.

### 2. Two arms, because one arm is defeatable (Ruling 140)

Each clause is cut at its first em-dash. The **head** is what the acceptance
asserts; the **tail** is how or why. That cut is this repository's own prose
convention (*"…— asserted, not assumed"*), not an invention.

| arm | what it refuses | why it exists |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | a bare cardinal ≥ 2, or *hundred*/*thousand*, governing a plural noun: `166 units`, `45 Java module pages` | the shape all four real instances had |
| **B** | *all/every/each/entire* over a corpus named by consumer role | ⛔ **all four instances carried a digit**, so a sweep built only from them finds only the half of its population that spells the count out |

⛔ **Cardinals belonging to identifiers are stripped first** — `Ruling 90`, `R7`,
`§4`, `SF-25`, `depth-2`, `1-level`, `16-digit` — or `Ruling 90` reads as ninety
of something. `test_the_anchor_vocabulary_does_not_swallow_a_real_count` asserts
that stripping in **both** directions, because an over-eager anchor would swallow
the class instead of the noise.

### 3. A tail magnitude is DECLARED, never ignored

The head cut is a real distinction and also a way through. Every magnitude found
in a **tail** is therefore declared in `DECLARED_TAIL_CITATIONS` with the reason
it is a citation and not a subject; an **undeclared** one fails. There is exactly
one today (`619 clips`, `E04`), and a second test asserts the declaration has not
outlived its subject. ⭐ This is the shape `tests/test_shape_vocabulary.py`
already uses for a divergence that is allowed only with its reason written down.

### 4. ⛔ `E02`'s restatement is carried VERBATIM, and it is another office's work

**Ruling 166 obligation 8 says `SF-07`'s clause is restated *"in the same edit as
`W70`'s sweep"*.** The PO had already written that restatement, on
`chore/po-round35`, which is **not merged**. Two bad options and one good one:
