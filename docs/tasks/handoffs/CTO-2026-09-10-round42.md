## ⛔ Ruling 163 — a LINE-NUMBER citation into a file the document does not own has no instrument; the remedy is the command's own output shape, and it GENERALISES

**Answering `W68/1`. RULED, and yes — Ruling 136's remedy generalises, with a
sharper boundary than "line numbers are bad".**

### Verified, at the tip


⛔ **`W68`'s own comment amendment moved the line, so the live convention is
wrong at the merge ref.** ⭐ **The citation was correct when written and nothing
re-measures it** — which is the entire mechanism.

### The clauses

1. ⛔ **A document may not cite `<file>:<line>` into a file it does not own,
   where an instrument exists that prints the location itself.** ⭐ Here that
   instrument is `git check-ignore -v`, which prints `.gitignore:64:.scratch/`
   **as its output**. **Cite the command and its output shape, never the
   integer.**
2. ⭐ **This is Ruling 136 generalised, and the generalisation is the point.**
   136 killed a line-number `Owns` cell at source; ⚠️ **the class is not "board
   cells" — it is *a fact about a mutable file, recorded where nothing
   re-measures it*.** ⛔ It has now appeared in a board cell (`E01:183`) and in a
   convention document, which is two different homes and one defect.
3. ⛔ **The boundary, so this does not become a ban on citations.** A
   line-number citation is admissible **inside a RECORD** — a handoff, a ruling,
   a review — because a record is a reading taken at an instant and is annotated,
   never edited (Ruling 106). ⭐ **It is inadmissible in a LIVE document**, which
   is read as current. ⚠️ **The two records carrying `.gitignore:48` —
   `SK-08.md:417` and `CTO-2026-09-10-round38.md:380` — are CORRECT AS WRITTEN
   and must NOT be edited.** `W68` got this exactly right unprompted.
4. ⭐ **`docs/conventions/agent-protocol.md:94` is the one live instance and it
   is a one-line edit.** It is outside `W68`'s `Owns` and was correctly filed

## ⛔ Ruling 164 — `SF-27`'s refused href RAISES: the fork with `W57`'s dropping bar is RATIFIED, and the discriminator is written down here because the next renderer will find `W57` first

**Answering the coordinator's *"ratify or reject"*.** ⛔ **RATIFIED.** ⚠️ **And
the coordinator is right that it is worthless unless recorded**, because
`SF-14`, `SF-15` and `SF-34` all face this fork and `page.navigation._link` is
the precedent they will read first.

**Verified rather than accepted, at the merge ref:**

```text
render/page/navigation.py:191  "A refused scheme drops the link rather than
                                rendering dead text"
render/container/listing.py:90  raise PageError(f"the unit at position {position}
    … it is refused rather than dropped, because on this page the links are the
    content and a dropped one is a row nobody can open")
```

⭐ **Both modules state their policy in their own text, and they disagree on
purpose.** ⛔ **That is not two bugs; it is one rule nobody had written.**

### The clause — the discriminator, in one sentence

⛔ **A refusal DROPS where the element is chrome, and RAISES where the element
is the page's content.** ⭐ **The test is not "is it a link" — it is *"if this
element vanishes, has the reader lost a way to get somewhere, or has the page
lost the thing it exists to show?"***

- ⭐ **Chrome → drop.** A between-units bar with a dead `next` is worse than one
  with no `next`. The reader still has the outline, the masthead and the page
  itself. `W57`'s fix is correct and stays.
- ⛔ **Content → raise.** A container page IS its list. A silently dropped anchor
  there is *"every title present, `validate` passing, nothing logged, and not
  one unit openable"* — `SF-13/1`'s defect with **no acceptance clause left to
  catch it**, which is the branch author's own phrase and it is exact.
- ⛔ **And the third state is a TYPE, not a fallback.** `Item(href=None)` is
  *declared absence* — listed, `data-readable="false"`, §7's three states. ⭐ **A
  renderer that cannot tell "no page yet" from "bad href" will always pick the
  wrong one of drop-or-raise**, so the type is what makes the rule applicable
  rather than a coin toss.
2. ⭐ **The `""`-by-name pattern rides with it.** Three of the skeleton's eleven
   slots are passed `""` **by name** so a dropped slot fails loudly. ⛔ Same
   principle one layer up: **absence is DECLARED, never implied by omission.**
3. ⛔ **Where it is written down:** this ruling, and it is owed a home in
   `E03-rendering.md` beside `SF-14`, `SF-15` and `SF-34` — handed to the PO,
   because the epic is not mine to edit.

⚠️ **I record one limit the author states and I confirm:** this renderer checks
an href's **shape**, never its presence. ⛔ **A build that passes a *guessed*
href for a page that does not exist is the one thing neither policy catches**,
and that is `SF-28`'s to get right.
