## Decisions

### ⛔ 1. The denominator the row names is NOT DECIDABLE, and that is a stronger result than *not readable*

⭐ **`CLAUDE.md` and `agent-protocol.md` both bind the obligation to DEPENDENTS.** The
protocol's own sentence, quoted from `docs/conventions/agent-protocol.md`: *"A task with
dependents and no handoff is not done."*

⛔ **A dependent can be minted AFTER the branch is gone.** This row is its own proof:
`docs/tasks/BOARD.md` places `W167` in *Next rows* at **round 54**, and the sentence that
makes `W64` a task with a dependent — *"`W64`+`W167` share ONE FILE, not merely a
directory"* — is attributed in that same table to **`PO-55/2`**, a round LATER still.
⚠️ **So at the moment `W64`'s obligation fell due, `W64` had no declared dependent at
all.** ⛔ **An instrument reading *has dependents* at any ref reads a population that
grows after the obligation has already been discharged or missed** — it is not a hard
read, it is a read of a fact that does not yet exist.

⭐ **That, and not a parsing difficulty, is why the reviewer had to remember.**

### ⛔ 2. And it is not READABLE either, measured

⚠️ **`**Depends on**` is an EPIC-document field, and the population that owes is `W` rows.**
Measured at `4b48e6d`, role `wt/dev3`, HOST, `grep -rn 'Depends on' docs/tasks/`: the
field appears in `docs/tasks/E00-foundations.md` … `E12-toolchain-image.md`, against
epic task ids (`FND-01`, `SF-02`); ⛔ **`docs/tasks/rows/` holds 122 files and NONE of
them carries the field** — 6 mention a hold or a dependency in PROSE, inside a cell or a
sentence. ⭐ **The relation an instrument would need does not exist in machine-readable
form for the population that owes it.**

### ⛔ 3. What a floor check may NOT read, and it forecloses the one instrument that could

⭐ **Ruling 80, quoted from `tools/quality/board/__init__.py`:** *"a floor check's verdict
may not depend on untracked state (Ruling 80), and a branch position is the purest
untracked state there is."* ⛔ **So *which row is in flight on this branch* is not
available to a floor check by rule, not merely by difficulty** — and `corroborate`, the
instrument that IS allowed to shell out to git, is not in `CHECKS` for exactly that
reason.

### ⛔ 4. `closed → owes` is REFUTED BY MEASUREMENT, and the measurement is the useful part

⚠️ **Instrument:** `tools/quality/board/register.py`'s own `register`/`is_closed`/`cells`
parsers, driven over `docs/tasks/BOARD.md`, against every `**Kind:** task handoff — …`
