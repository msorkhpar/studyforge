## 7. Rulings this round mints

### ⛔ Ruling 180 — a MIGRATION test's subject is the migration's OUTPUT at a named ref, never the live tree

> ⭐ **A migration is an event. Its claim is *this content went there*, and it is
> true or false of two REFS.** ⛔ **A test that reads the destination from the
> working tree has silently made the claim *this content is STILL there,
> unedited* — which is a freeze, and a freeze is the opposite of what a live
> half is for.**

⚠️ **`CTO-46/1` is the instance.** ⛔ **The equality is the right property; the
subject is wrong, and the tell is that the document shipped beside it TELLS the
reader to edit the very files the test pins.** ⭐ **The pass condition: read both
sides from `git show <ref>:<path>`, and let the unreachable-ref reading SKIP.**

⛔ **The corollary, and it is the half that gets skipped:** ⭐ **a test that
forbids an action the branch's own contract PRESCRIBES is a finding against the
test, every time.** ⚠️ **Read the contract you are shipping and plant its four
documented actions against your own instrument.**

**Artifact:** `docs/conventions/review-rubric.md` §9, beside Ruling 177 — the two
are one clause: **validate a migration over CONTENT (177), at a REF (180).**
