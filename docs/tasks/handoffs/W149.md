## What landed

- `tools/quality/subject.py` is a pre-merge predicate over ONE subject string. Run it as `python3 -m tools.quality.subject "<subject>"`, or pass the subject on stdin. It sets aside the header and a `(CTO: …)` bracket, ENUMERATES the claims in the prose, and prints each one as `PROPERTY` or `READING`. ## Decisions

- ⭐ **A command, not a floor check.** A subject is not in the tree, so `CHECKS` cannot see it. A `NOTICES` entry over `git log` would be the sweep of history that the row forbids. ⛔ **So `tools/quality/__init__.py` is untouched, and the registry cannot conflict with `W156`.**
- ⭐ **A READING is decided as a claim that carries a bare count.** That covers `CTO-63/6`'s integer and `CTO-66/19`'s number word. Names are not counts: identifiers, dates, and numerals after a `NAMING` word. Everything the predicate does not decide is DECLARED in the module docstring, and each declared shape is asserted silent.
- A claim ends at `;` or at a spaced dash. A comma does not end a claim, so a count is never lost to a split.
- A subject with no `Merge <branch>` header is read whole. No shape rule was added that nobody ruled.
