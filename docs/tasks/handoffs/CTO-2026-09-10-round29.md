## ⛔ ITEM 1 — the ruling on the fourth marker

### ⭐ The short answer

⛔ **`[negative]` does not join the registry (Ruling 105).** ⭐ **But a recorded
negative IS a finding**, and it is written as a `[local]` finding whose text opens
with `A NEGATIVE result.` — ⚠️ **which is exactly what my predecessor did to their
own two before handing the question on, so the worked example already exists in
the tree at `CTO-28/5` and `CTO-28/6`.**

⛔ **And the code change lands NOWHERE**, because refusing the marker is a
zero-diff outcome in `tools/quality/handoffs/contract.py`, in `review-rubric.md`
§8a, and in every test. ⭐ **That resolves the sequencing question against
Developer 2 by dissolving it rather than by scheduling around it.**

### ⛔ Why — and the premise I had to correct first

⚠️ **`CTO-28/7` is CORRECT that the three findings are invisible and WRONG about
the cause, and the difference changes the answer.** Measured at `6e80c82`:

```text
tools/quality/handoffs/__init__.py     if kind != TASK_HANDOFF: continue
                                       → check_markers() never runs otherwise

docs/tasks/handoffs/ kind census       40 task handoff · 38 ruling record
                                       3 survey · 2 session log · 1 index  = 84

Kind of all three [negative] carriers  ruling record · ruling record · ruling record
```

⛔ **All three carriers are ruling records, and `check_markers` does not run on a
ruling record at all.** ⚠️ **So the marker was never the reason.** Run both ways,
the registration is a measured no-op:

```text

check_handoffs(".")  after  registering the marker  →  0 findings
tools/tests/quality  with the fourth marker added   →  281 passed, 1 skipped
```

⭐ **The last row is its own finding: nothing pins the vocabulary.** A fourth
marker can be added today and the entire quality suite stays green.

### ⛔ The substantive question: is a recorded negative a finding?

⭐ **Yes.** ⛔ **§8a's premise — *a finding IS a marked item* — is a statement
about REPRESENTABILITY, not about desert.** The counter counts marked items so
that an unmarked one is unrepresentable rather than silently uncounted. ⚠️ **A
negative that was investigated, concluded and recorded so nobody re-raises it is
an item a reviewer must read and account for** — ⭐ **and all three existing ones
already carry scoped `<TASK-ID>/<n>` ids, which is their own authors treating
them as findings.** ⛔ **A round that disproves three suspicions has not produced
fewer findings than a round that confirms them.**

### ⛔ So why refuse the marker, if the negative is a finding?

⚠️ **Not for Ruling 65's reason, and a successor must not cite it for this.**
⛔ **Ruling 65 forbids widening the SPELLINGS of one marker** — an open-set
failure, because formatting cannot be enumerated. ⭐ **Adding a fourth closed word
with exactly one spelling is not that mistake**, and refusing it on those grounds
would be a misreading.

⛔ **The reason is that a marker encodes ROUTING, and a negative's routing is
identical to `[local]`'s: nowhere.** ⭐ **The fourth word would carry zero routing
information and one new failure mode** — ⚠️ **and that failure mode is measured,
not hypothetical: one of its three uses had already done it.**

⛔ **`PO-24/2` is filed under a marker meaning *no action owed* and its own text
says *"The fix is unchanged"* and names the remedy.** ⭐ **A marker that lets a
live structural finding read as a no-op is an attractive nuisance**, and a 1-in-3
misuse rate before the word was even registered is the strongest evidence
available about what it would do next. ⚠️ **Polarity is CONTENT. Triage is
routing. The two do not belong in the same field.**

### ⚠️ What is deliberately NOT done, and why

⛔ **The three existing uses are NOT re-marked.** ⭐ **They live in records, and
Ruling 106 — minted in this same round — says a record is annotated, never
edited.** ⚠️ **Ordering the three rewritten would have contradicted my own ruling
in the document that makes it.** This handoff is the annotation; the ruling is
prospective; ⛔ **and `PO-24/2`'s live half is re-routed below as `CTO-29/3` so
that nothing is lost by leaving the record alone.**
