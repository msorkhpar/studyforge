## Decisions

- ⭐ **"New" is a REF.** A handoff is new when it is absent from the tree at `4d3c742` (`4d3c74218609971e076535416064b6dffa6f9440`), the release tip this rule lands on.
  - ⚠️ **A directory cannot separate the two.** All 306 documents under `docs/tasks/handoffs/` at the pin are frozen, and a new handoff lands beside them.
  - ⚠️ **A wave marker cannot either.** None exists, and a declared field is escaped by leaving it out.
  - ⭐ **A pinned tree is immutable and names no file.** ⛔ It is a pin, not a knob.
- ⛔ **If git cannot read the pin, the arm fails CLOSED.** Every handoff is read as new, and the notice says `could NOT be read`.
- **Why an arm:** `tools/quality/handoffs/` is not at R11's ceiling. The largest module, `contract.py`, is at 360 of 400, so the arm is a sibling module rather than a split.
- **Only task and office handoffs are bound.** Those are the two kinds the six-section contract binds; see `W135/3`.

## Surprises

- ⚠️ **The row's population has moved.** At `b182a88`, 226 of 306 handoffs carry no markdown pointer, and all 226 are frozen. With no exemption, the arm would fire in 131 of the 159 task and office handoffs.
- ⭐ **`git ls-tree` on the pin works inside the pinned image, from a linked worktree.**
- ⚠️ **This handoff went RED on its own rule first**, three times. A filename discussed AS A STRING still resolves by its tail to a tracked row file, and a quoted bare citation is still a citation. ⭐ Both were reworded, and the floor then read `0 of 1`.
