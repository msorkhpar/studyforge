## Decisions

- ⛔ **The sort is written INTO the catalogue, not only into this handoff.** ⭐ **The catalogue's own
  principle decided it:** *a contribution silently not adopted is indistinguishable from one nobody
  read*, and it already writes its refusals beside its adoptions. ⚠️ **A sort that lived only in a
  handoff would leave the next integrator re-proposing all thirteen refusals**, which is the exact
  cost R19 exists to stop.
- ⛔ **A NEW dated section rather than an edit to the 2026-09-10 one.** ⭐ That section is a landed
  record of a particular run of wave check 6 (Ruling 106); appending this run's dispositions to it
  would make one dated reading carry two.
- ⚠️ **Entry 23 states BOTH numbers and asserts no cause.** ⛔ **The measurement is above the top of
  the estimate this file already carried, and the obvious sentence — *the estimator under-predicts* —
  is a claim I did not measure.** ⭐ It is filed as a finding for whoever owns that estimate
  (`QA-04/6`), and the entry carries only what a planner can act on.
- ⛔ **The pin accounting refuses the clause's literal form and discharges its intent.** ⭐ The clause
  wants *no commit crossed without a reason*; the reason here is one intention covering all 43, and
  the honest discharge is to name the intention, count the commits, and attribute the three rows a
  conversion finding actually forced. ⚠️ **Listing 43 subject lines against a finding that does not
  exist would have satisfied the sentence and told a reader nothing.**
- ⭐ **Two findings are named as UNFILED HOLES rather than turned into catalogue entries**
  (`QA-04/2`, `QA-04/4`). ⛔ Filing either here is precisely the move R19 forbids, and minting a row
  is the register's, not mine.
- ⚠️ **The register's *ten rows merged* was not inherited.** ⭐ It is a true statement about the WAVE;
  the pin range holds nine, measured. ⛔ **The two populations are different and this handoff says
  which it is quoting** (`QA-04/8`).
