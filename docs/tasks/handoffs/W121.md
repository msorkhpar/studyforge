one vocabulary* defect Ruling 193 names, at a third site. `W121/6` below is the
test that holds that shut.

The module docstring gained the `W121` section and the `LEAD_TOKENS` comment was
corrected: it claimed a `|` was in no token and that a table row was therefore
refused, which stopped being true. `contract.py` is 314 lines of 400;
`test_contract.py` is 444 of 600. Neither needed a split (R11).
