# ⛔ Ruling 102 — `SF-36` mints `container_api: 2`, and the escalated question had a FALSE PREMISE

⛔ **The question was *"does `SF-36` ride inside `SF-35`'s `corpus_api: 2`, or


⛔ **`origin` is a `container.json` field. It appears nowhere in the manifest
package.** ⭐ **So the register that moves is `container_api`, by Ruling 95's own
convention: a located contract takes its own filename's noun.**

## ⭐ The PO's reasoning was right in SHAPE and wrong in SET, which is the part worth carrying

⚠️ **Everything they argued holds.** R9's rule — *a document USING the new shape
must be unreadable to a build that does not speak it* — applied correctly; *two
additive shapes in two commits take two versions* is correct; and they escalated
rather than guessing, which is why this was caught before a developer spent a
25k budget in the wrong package. ⛔ **What was wrong is which register the second
version lives in**, and nothing in their chain of reasoning could have surfaced
it, because the chain never asked *which file carries the key*.

⭐ **This is Ruling 81 arriving in a version register:** the answer reproduced
the right *shape* — two shapes, two commits, two numbers — while its *set* was
wrong. ⚠️ **A matching shape is evidence that the shape matches, and nothing
more.**

> ⛔ **So the rule, and it costs one sentence: a contract version is minted in
> the register of the DOCUMENT that carries the new key. Name the file the key
> appears in before naming the version.**

2. ⛔ **`SF-36`'s `Owns` was WRONG** — it read `corpus/manifest/` and the shape
