## Decisions

- ⛔ **`--voice` is required with no default.** The manifest declares no voice and
  `Conditions` refuses an unstated one. `--format` defaults to `mp3` (the
  service's closed set); `--service` to `http://127.0.0.1:8870`.
- ⛔ **An absent service exits `2`**, not `1`: nothing about the corpus is wrong.
  A service lost mid-corpus exits `1` with earlier clips placed and recorded.
- ⭐ **Probe once; the re-run assertion is stricter than the row** — not just no
  ids submitted, but no request other than the one probe.
- **`synth.audio_dir` is used despite `SF-42/1`**, so this writer and the build's
  reader stay on one spelling.
- ⛔ **The end-to-end leg is gated on `STUDYFORGE_NARRATION_SERVICE`.** Unset: it
  skips with a `DID NOT RUN` reason `pytest -ra` prints. Set: the service must
  answer — an unreachable URL FAILS, never skips.
