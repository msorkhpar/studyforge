## The design

- ⭐ **An aspect is one important idea a page teaches that a reader could be checked on.** It is
  read from the page's prose (`section:<heading>`) or its code (`example:<path>:<n>`,
  `tests:<path>`, the page's own ledger keys). ⛔ **It ends one of two ways**: the name of the
  planned exercise that checks it, or a written reason nothing does. An aspect with neither is
  refused, and so is one with both. That is the ledger's own rule for files (`accounting`),
  applied to ideas.
- ⭐ **The plan is read off the names.** It has one planned exercise per distinct `exercise` name,
  and each exercise carries the aspects it checks. So one exercise checking several related
  aspects is the natural shape, as the refinement asks. An exercise that checks nothing cannot
  be planned. ⛔ **No length ceiling and no quota.** `shortfall` is unchanged (R6).
- ⛔ **Near-duplicates are refused.** Since an aspect names one exercise, two exercises cannot
  check one aspect. The remaining route is one idea under two ids, and its mechanical half is
  refused: one id twice, or one sentence twice once case, spacing and punctuation are set aside.
  A restatement in other words is the author's judgement to catch, and the module says so.
- ⛔ **Zero is legitimate and never silent.** If every aspect is reasoned, the plan is zero with a
  reason per aspect. A page naming no aspect must write `nothing_checkable`, and a page naming
  some must not.
- ⭐ **A thin plan is visible.** The coverage report's plan lists every aspect with its ending,
  `Plan.reasoned` lists the unchecked ones, and `Authored.reasoned` lists them for the whole pass,
  page by page.
- ⭐ **R10:** aspects are held in id order and exercises in name order, so the order the author
  listed them in cannot move the plan. ⭐ **R1:** nothing names a source. The finding id
  `ISO-M10/6` is cited as history in two docstrings, the same way `loop.py` already cites the
  first corpus.

## Findings

### W453/1 `[structural]`: an aspect's link to its exercise is recorded, not gated

⚠️ The plan says exercise `field` checks aspects `reads-a-field` and `missing`, and the brief hands
the author both. **No gate reads whether the shipped exercise's cases actually exercise them.**
The gates prove the exercise is sound (`G1`–`G5`, `Q1`–`Q5`), not that it covers its aspects. A
future gate could require every checked aspect to be named by at least one case's or question's
`says`. That is a judgement-shaped check, so this row records it and does not build it.

### W453/2: the near-duplicate refusal is literal

Only the same sentence under two ids is refused. *"A basket totals its prices"* and *"the sum of
a basket's prices is its total"* pass. The module says so, and review is what catches it.

### W453/3: records that still teach the band, left unedited

`docs/tasks/handoffs/AX-07.md` teaches `plan_for(words=…, skills=…)` and the band's property. The
ISO corpus's `docs/studyforge/m10-plan.md` §1.3 (the `band` column) and its `ISO-23` Definition
(*"Three units plan zero by their band"*) do too. The first is a record. The second belongs to
the ISO office, and `ISO-23` below says what changes.

### W453/4: `words_of` sets nothing now

It is exported and tested, and the framework no longer uses it for any count. Keep it as a
reading, or retire it: the register decides. Nothing breaks either way.
