- the control is at `top 1528, bottom 1568` in a `900`px viewport at rest, which is

- the computed `scroll-behavior` on `html` is `smooth` (`reset.css`);
- `scrollY` read straight after the traversal is `0`, and then across frames it reads

⭐ **So focus-scroll into view is a smooth GLIDE**, and the old `left = _state(...)` ran

say `0`. The panel remembers `was = pageYOffset` at the press, and `Enter` ends the glide.

⭐ **Reading 2: under load, every drift is a glide frame.** A scratch repeater (not

## Decisions

- ⭐ **The test was fixed, not the product.** The product restores exactly the position
  it had at the press. What moved was the harness's reading of "the place the reader
  left", which it took of a page in motion. A reader who sees the control has
  already seen the glide finish.
- ⭐ **`scrollend` and not a frame-stability heuristic.** The glide's first frames can read
  `0, 0` (measured `0` then `2`), so "unchanged for N frames" could return before the
  glide had begun. `scrollend` is the browser's own signal that the scroll has finished.
  It is armed before the traversal, so a glide that finishes early is still recorded.
  It is gated on the control being in view, so the rest of an earlier stop in the
  traversal cannot answer.
- ⚠️ **Not `prefers-reduced-motion`.** Emulating it would remove the glide, but the check
  would then read a configuration other than the default the gate exists for.

## Surprises

- The row names a font or a late layout as candidates. Neither is the cause. The glide
  is the same mechanism `W431/1` and `W431/2` repaired in the product and in the scroll
  case's setup (`behavior: 'instant'`). This time it lived in the **focus** scroll that
  a Tab press causes, which no `scrollTo` argument can reach.
- The whole-suite `-n auto` alone did not reproduce it on this host (one run, before),
  and neither did five visual-suite runs. The repeater beside the whole suite did.

## Findings

- `[local]` **`W441/1` (the product, not fixed): the restore can leave the focused control off
  screen.** If *Maximise* is pressed mid-glide, `was` is a mid-glide position.
  Escape then restores to it and focuses the control with `preventScroll: true`. With a
  press at glide frame 0, the page returns to `0` and the focused control sits at
  `1528` in a `900`px viewport. It is only reachable by pressing within about 560ms of
  tabbing, and the equality this row guards still holds. ⚠️ It is a question for the
  register: whether the restore should also bring the control into view.
- `[local]` **`W441/2` (test, not fixed): `test_restoring_gives_back_the_place_in_the_page_the_reader_left`
  cannot see a small overshoot.** Its reader is at the foot of the page. The plant
  `was + 4` is clamped by the browser back to the maximum scroll, so that case stayed
  GREEN under the plant, and only the case this row repaired went RED. It still reads
  the clamp it was written for (the 306px defect). The same case also runs `TO_THE_END`
  while `_tab_to`'s glide may be in flight. It relies on the instant `scrollTo` aborting
  the glide, which held in every run here.
