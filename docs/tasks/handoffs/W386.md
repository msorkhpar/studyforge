## What landed

- `cli.serve.site_namespaces(discovered, content)` returns the `--site` form's namespaces
  (`state` and `run`) from `serve.instance.namespaces_of`. The verb calls it once per `--site`
  serve and registers as writers only the members of `WRITERS` it returned.
- The skill's test replaces `site_namespaces` with a plant that records its call and returns
  nothing. It asserts that the plant was called once, that `run` is not registered, that no
  namespace writes, and that the skill reports `toolchain`.

## Decisions

- ⭐ **A public name on the verb, not a new parameter.** The test runs the verb through the
  skill's `build_and_serve` (`src/studyforge/skills/buildserve/run.py`, outside this surface),
  so a keyword on `main` would not reach it without editing the skill. A public module name is a
  seam the test can plant without reaching into a private one.
- ⭐ **The plant must be REACHED.** A replaced function that the verb stopped calling would
  leave `run` registered, and the test would already fail on that. The recorded call also
  names why, rather than leaving a reader to infer it from a namespace assertion.
