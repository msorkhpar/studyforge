## The defect, and the part worth more than it

```text
aria-label  "Two nodes and an arrow"          the SVG's own accessible name
alt         "Two nodes joined by one arrow"   the lesson's, for the same image
geometry    <line x1 y1 x2 y2 stroke=…>       no marker, no arrowhead, no arrow
```

⛔ **Three descriptions of one image and no two agreed** — and the thing all
three describe was not drawn. A screen reader and a sighted reader were handed
different pictures of something absent.

⭐ **Found by a graph build, not by a test, and that is the finding.** Every
check that touches this file passed: the digest matched, the byte count matched,
the media check found the file on disk, the block shape was valid. ⚠️ **None of
them can read a picture**, and none of them compares an accessible name to what
it names — so the fixture was *consistent* and *wrong*, which is the state
FND-04's fixtures exist to make impossible for everything else.

## What changed

- The `<line>` carries `marker-end="url(#arrowhead)"`, with a `<marker>` in
  `<defs>` — the standard construction, no library and no external reference
  (R8: it must render from `file://`).
- Both accessible names read **"Two nodes joined by one arrow"** — the lesson's
  wording, which is the more descriptive of the two.
- ⛔ **Four digests moved, and none by hand**: the file's `sha256` and `bytes`,
  the lesson's `assets_sha256` (which covers them), and `content_sha256` (which
  covers the `alt` text). All recomputed with `tests.fixture_checks.corpus.sha256_of`
  — the same function the check uses.

⚠️ **Recomputing by hand would have been the trap.** `digests.py` says it
outright: `tests/fixtures/invalid/digest-mismatch/` exists to prove that check
fires, and a helper that "repaired" a digest while walking the tree would
silently make that fixture valid. The recompute here is a one-off script over
one document, not a repair pass.

## Findings

### 1. `[structural]` Nothing in the fixture checks compares an accessible name to what it names

⛔ **The class, not the instance.** `check_media` verifies a declared file
exists at the recorded size and digest; nothing asks whether an `alt` and an
`aria-label` for the same image agree, and nothing could have caught that the
arrow was not drawn.

⭐ **The cheap half is available and the expensive half is not.** *"An image
block's `alt` and its SVG's `aria-label` say the same thing"* is a string
comparison over a derived pair and would have caught two of the three
disagreements here. *"The picture contains what the name says"* is not
mechanisable and stays a review question.

⚠️ Filed rather than built: W3 was slack behind two ruled tasks, and a new
fixture check is a task, not a ride-along. **This is the one to schedule if
W14 is adding fixtures** — a second image fixture authored under the same blind
spot inherits it.

### 2. `[local]` The accessible names were not the only pair with no owner

The lesson's `alt` lives in `raw/prose/unit-02/lesson-1.json` and the
`aria-label` lives in the SVG two directories away, under `units/`. ⚠️ Nothing
links them but the `src` path, so the two are edited by different tasks at
different times with nothing failing in between. That is the mechanism behind
finding 1, stated as a fact about the layout rather than about this file.

## For dependents

- **`tests/fixtures/depth1/.../unit-02/lesson-1.json` changed its digests.**
  Any branch that recorded the old `content_sha256` or `assets_sha256` will
  conflict, and the resolution is to recompute rather than to pick a side.
- ⛔ **Do not hand-edit a digest in this tree.** `sha256_of` is in
  `tests/fixture_checks/corpus.py`, and the invalid fixture that proves the
  check fires is one directory away.
