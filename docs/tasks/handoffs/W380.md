## What landed

- `serve.instance.namespaces_of(discovered, sources)` returns the `state` and `run`
  namespaces over a discovery. `instance_of` (the root form) calls it over every corpus it
  discovered. `cli/serve.py` (the `--site` form) calls it over its one corpus. `WRITERS` is
  the one spelling of which namespaces answer `POST`.
- `serve.instance.site_discovery(corpus, root, site)` is the one-corpus discovery that
  `--site` serves. Its corpus is a `SiteCorpus`, a `ServedCorpus` whose `rescan` scans the
  **site**, not the corpus root. The progress store and the unit documents stay at the
  corpus root. Its startup scan judges no cache and it reports nothing, so nothing is written
  into the site or the corpus root.

## Decisions

- ⭐ **One constructor, not a truthful docstring.** `W371/1` offered both. A docstring that
  says where each form registers its namespaces would still leave two authors of the same
  wiring, and `W371/2` shows how that goes wrong: one form gained a namespace and the other
  did not. With one constructor, a namespace added to `namespaces_of` reaches both forms
  without anyone editing the verb.
- ⭐ **The site is scanned where it is built.** Registering `state` alone was not enough.
  `W371`'s one-corpus discovery scanned the corpus root, and when a site is built with `--out`
  elsewhere, the root holds no pages. `state` believes a recorded practice only for a unit
  whose page is present, so every page would have been reported absent and every run's
  record reported as a disagreement. `SiteCorpus` overrides only `rescan`. It lives in
  `serve/instance.py` because `serve/discovery.py` is outside this row's surface (see
  `W380/2`).
- ⚠️ **Three names were exported from `studyforge.serve`, one file outside the literal
  surface.** The floor's `surface-reached-past` check refused `cli/serve.py` taking them from
  `studyforge.serve.instance`. The check's stated remedy is one line on the package's
  `__all__`, and adding them to `DECLARED` is forbidden (`W199`). This is the same remedy
  `W371` took for `Discovered` and `ServedCorpus`. Now that `serve/__init__.py` imports
  `serve.instance`, importing the package loads the run route too. The package's own
  import-graph tests in `tests/studyforge/serve/test_init.py` pass.
- ⚠️ **The seam is still named `_execution`.** The build-and-serve skill's test
  (`tests/studyforge/skills/buildserve/test_run.py`, outside this surface) replaces
  `cli.serve._execution` to serve a site with no execution. The function now returns both
  namespaces, so its writers are derived as `WRITERS` filtered to what it returned. An empty
  replacement therefore still binds (see `W380/1`).

## Surprises

- The first reading of the settling clause, *"answers the state namespace"*, would have
  passed with `state` registered over the `W371` discovery: `/api/v1/state/` answered 200.
  The unit's answer was wrong, though: `present: false`, and the passing Submit came back as
  a disagreement. `test_a_page_missing_from_the_site_is_absent_although_the_corpus_root_holds_it`
  plants exactly this. In a temp copy with `rescan` pointed back at the root, it fails, along
  with the read-back test and the `site_discovery` unit test.

## For dependents

- ⭐ **On a `--site` origin, `/api/v1/state/` and `/api/v1/state/{corpus}/units/{key}` answer as
  on a root origin.** A unit's `pages` are hrefs into the site, and a practice a run recorded
  appears under `practices`. Only `discovery.cache` differs: the site form judges no
  `site.json`.
- Adding a namespace to a served instance means adding it to `namespaces_of` (and, if it
  writes, to `WRITERS`). Both forms then serve it.
- `W372` (a served-route container test) can rely on `state` being present in both forms.
