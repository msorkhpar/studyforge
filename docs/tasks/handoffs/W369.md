## What landed

- ⭐ **Clause 1 — a wide window uses its width.** The index's explanation (its lede and its two
  short columns) moved out of the top of `main` into the skeleton's aside slot, and a third grid
  column, `--aside`, carries it beside the list where the window has room. The list keeps the
  reading column, and the prose measure is untouched by it. The unit page's outline moved the
  same way for the same reason, which is `W388`'s clause 4.
- ⭐ **Clause 2 — nothing narration-related is lit before narration starts.** `narration.js` no
  longer lights the first passage at load; the transport's own line still says where narration
  will begin, and `load()` — which a press is the only route to — is what lights a passage.

## Decisions

- ⭐ **The fix is in the script, not in a CSS state.** *"Lit at rest"* was a real attribute on a
  real passage, and the honest reading of the row is that the attribute should not be there
  before a press. The paused mark in the margin stays, for a reader who pressed play and paused.
