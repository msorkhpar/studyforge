## For dependents

- ⛔ **`branch_exists`, `checkouts`, `ahead`, `merged` and `merge_of` ARE GONE from
  `tools.quality.board.corroborate`.** ⭐ **They are `Graph`'s methods now** —
  `exists`, `checkouts`, `ahead`, `merged`, and `merge_of` split into `terminal`
  (the gate, off the graph) and `named` (the printed ref, off the subject).
  ⚠️ **`tokens` is `verdict.py`'s and is still re-exported through `corroborate`.**
- ⛔ **A TERMINAL branch now REFUTES even while a checkout holds it.** ⭐ **So a
  developer whose own branch has merged and who keeps their worktree will see their row
  refuted at the next close, correctly** — ⚠️ **and the fix for that reading is to
  close the row, not to remove the worktree.**
- ⭐ **Shape `B`'s exact match means a merge message MUST be spelled
  `Merge <branch>: <line> (CTO: …)` for the printed ref to appear.** ⛔ **The gate does
  not depend on it** — `C` reads the graph — ⚠️ **but a merge whose subject drops the
  colon prints `message B none` and a DISAGREE line, which is noise a coordinator can
  avoid for free by keeping the idiom.**
- ⚠️ **`C` cannot see a FAST-FORWARDED merge**, measured at `2d0cfe7` as `7` real
  branches: `chore/po-round18`, `chore/po-round21`, `chore/po-round22`,
  `feat/FND-09-sweep`, `feat/QA-03-visual`, `feat/ruling-78-lint-notice`,
  `fix/W30-W31`. ⭐ **Those degrade to the ancestor arm and are still refuted** when
  `0` ahead and unheld; the gap only matters for a branch somebody also keeps checked
  out. ⛔ **A `--no-ff` merge is what makes this instrument sharp, and the verdict
  convention already requires one.**
- ⛔ **The residual risk `C` carries, stated because nothing else states it:** a branch
  cut from ANOTHER branch's already-absorbed tip reads terminal. ⭐ **Such a branch is
  `0` ahead and carries nothing of its own, so refuting it is correct** — ⚠️ **and a
  branch cut from the release tip CANNOT hit it, because the release tip is on the
  first-parent line by construction.**
