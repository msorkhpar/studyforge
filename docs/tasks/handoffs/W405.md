### `W405/2` `[method]` — `W398/4`'s stated mechanism is not what the instruments do

⛔ **Measured at this row's ref, pinned image.** `W398/4` and this row's own brief both say
the lint gate *stops at the first* finding, and that is why a refusal names one. ⚠️ **It
does not.** `ruff check` reports every finding in one run; the test makes one assertion and
the whole of ruff's output is in its message. ⭐ **Three findings planted across two tracked
files produced `1 failed` — ONE test — with all three named inside its failure block**, and
the only thing that showed one was pytest's `short test summary info` line, which elides the
message with `- ...`. ⛔ **What genuinely stops at the first is `tools.mergegate`, which
stops at the first red GATE** and names the gates not taken — a different claim with a
different remedy (re-take the whole certification, not the one arm that spoke).
⭐ **[`agent-protocol.md`](../../conventions/agent-protocol.md) now carries the measured mechanism.** ⚠️ **[`rows/W405.md`](../rows/W405.md) and
the register's dispatch text still carry the original wording**; both are the register's to
correct, and the practical instruction they give — *read the file whole* — is unaffected and
still right.
