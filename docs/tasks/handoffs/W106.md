## Decisions

- ⭐ **A weak pattern is one property of one spelling:** an escaped bracket around a marker's
  name, without the backticks. It is not a list of shapes. The one excuse is a ruled twin in
  the same fence, because that pair is Ruling 193's measurement of the mentions delta.
- **Population is `docs/conventions/` only.** Handoffs and the archive are records (Ruling 106),
  and `docs/tasks/rows/` is out of scope.
- **The command lives in `__main__`.** The floor imports `sweep`, so `python3 -m ...sweep`
  printed a runpy `RuntimeWarning` (measured). `test_main.py` runs the real command.
