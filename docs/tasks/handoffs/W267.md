## Decisions

- ⭐ **Verbs, not a shorter list.** Dropping paths from the plan would move the footprint, `validate`'s recognition of generated output and the scaffold's globs. Reversible: remove the three fields and `verb`.
- ⭐ **A claimed media directory, not a copy of the build's list.** `plan` opens no unit document, and which directories a build fills is decided by the references inside them (`W267/1`).
- ⚠️ **A clip at the corpus root is `narrate`'s**, so its line is `keep` or `expect`. A build into any other output still copies it, and the line says so.

## Surprises

- The `generate` golden readers and `test_clips.py` selected `create ` lines. Their subject was every path, and `create` was the only path verb, so they would have hidden a verb change as silently as the golden hid `W268/1` (`W267/2`).

## For dependents

- **A reader of `plan`'s text:** a path line starts with one of `create`, `replace`, `keep`, `claim` or `expect`, the tuple `CREATION_VERBS`. Read `Plan.paths` or `Plan.creations` rather than grepping `create `.
