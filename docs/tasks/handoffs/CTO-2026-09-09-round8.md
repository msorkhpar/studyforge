### The fixture-vs-manifest decision — ⭐ CONFIRMED, and it moved the right side

⚠️ **You were right to flag that the author of both sides judged its own
artifacts**, so I checked the reasoning rather than the outcome.

**The manifest is right and the fixtures were wrong.** `content` cannot have a
permissive default: "everything is included" *is* C2's double ingest, written as
a default. It cannot sensibly default to "nothing" either — that makes an empty
corpus indistinguishable from a valid one. ⭐ `content` is not a policy knob with
a natural default; it is a statement about the material that only the corpus
author can make, which is precisely the class of field that must be **required**.

And the direction of pressure is the important part: ⛔ **fixtures serve the
contract, never the reverse.** A task that had bent the manifest to avoid editing
five fixtures would have shipped C2 to keep its test data still.

**Verified independently:** all five `invalid/` corpora carry a minimal `content`
block, and `test_fixture_consistency.py` is **22 passed** — the *exactly one
violation* property that `SF-25`'s acceptance rests on is intact.

### Decision 6 — no `reverse` field · ⭐ CONFIRMED

**Derived, not declared.** An `insert-line` entry already records `path`,
`anchor` and `content` exactly, so its inverse is fully determined. A `reverse`
field would be a **second record of one fact**, which this project has refused
three times already — §8.2's second clip-name minter, §6's two records naming an
address, `is_ignored` duplicated across two test files.

⚠️ **R3 says the reverse is *recorded*, and derived still satisfies that — under
one condition, which `SF-02` already meets.** What R3 is buying is that an
onboarding can be undone by someone who wants to read what will happen first. So:

- ⛔ **`EDIT_KINDS` stays closed**, and every kind in it has a defined inverse. An
  unknown kind is refused at parse time rather than guessed at. **Verified:**
  `EDIT_KINDS = ("insert-line",)`, unknown kinds refused.
- ⭐ **The uninstall prints what it will do before doing it**, like `studyforge
  plan`. *Derived* must not mean *invisible*.

⚠️ Widening `EDIT_KINDS` is where this ruling is tested: a kind whose inverse is
not determined by its own fields may not be added without either a `reverse`
field for that kind or a refusal.

## Ruling — R7 in exception text · ⛔ build it now, and the urgency argument is right

⭐ **Agreed, and the argument quoted back at me is mine and still correct: a check
that arrives after twenty sites exist is one nobody turns on.** One site today;
`SF-04`, `SF-25`, `SF-28` and every adapter will each add more.
