## Decisions

- **The existing `depth2` was extended, rather than a fourth corpus added.** Five helpers fix
  their fixture set as `depth1`/`depth2`: `sites.FIXTURES`, `containers.FIXTURES`,
  `indexes.FIXTURES`, `cli/serving.NAMES` and `generate/corpora.BOTH`. A new corpus would
  reach only the modules parametrised over `VALID`, and none of the walk, bar or index tests.
  Extending `depth2` puts the crossing in front of every one of them. The cost was literal
  `depth2` shapes, all attributable (below).
- **The new module sorts between the other two.** `advanced/03-…` falls between `advanced/02-…`
  and `basics/01-…`, so its crossings involve no unit without a page. The last unit, the one
  with no page, stays `basics/01-getting-started/unit-03`, so the walk tests' skip of absent
  units never hides the new crossing.
- **`PLANTED_SHAPE` is kept.** It is the one module-only crossing under `tree` placement, and
  `depth2` is `sibling`. Its duplicate definition in `sites.py` was removed, and both
  docstrings that said "neither fixture has it" were corrected.

## Surprises

- **Plant 3 was not a discriminating control.** It rendered any `../x/…` href as `x/…`, which
  also broke `tree` placement's `../unit-NN/…` hrefs, so depth1 went red with or without the
  fixture. Plant 4 narrows it to a step between modules alone, and that is the plant of record.
- **The `SF-15` walk tests catch a module-only crossing only if the fixture has one.** With plant
  4 applied and the new container removed, every depth2 walk and link test survives. With the
  container present, they go RED.
