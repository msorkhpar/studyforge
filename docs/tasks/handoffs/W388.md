## What landed

### 1. A palette for hours of reading, both themes

Light is a warm off-white paper with a soft warm-grey ink; dark is a warm charcoal with a
parchment-grey ink. Text is never pure black on light and never pure white on dark. Every line
and every accent at rest is low-chroma: the margin rule is a neutral hairline, and the one
accent is a muted ink blue that still means only *next* or *you are here*.

## Decisions

- ⭐ **The aside is the skeleton's `outline` slot, for both page kinds.** The index puts its
  explanation there rather than at the top of `main`, so one CSS rule places the secondary block
  of every page kind and `main` keeps being the reading column exactly as the column check reads
  it.
- ⭐ **No second width threshold.** `chrome.css` still declares one (`72rem`), which the browser
  arm reads back; at that width the prose column is a little narrower than `--measure` and
  reaches it as the window widens. A second threshold would have split *wide* into two layouts
  that the rail modules measure as one.
- ⭐ **The trail keeps the FIRST of two crumbs with one title.** The root crumb is where every
  trail starts, and the rail still reaches the group's own page.
- ⭐ **`main > :first-child` has no top margin.** Without it the flow shape and the grid shape
  disagreed about where the reading starts, because a first heading's margin collapses out
  through `main` in flow and does not in a grid.
- ⭐ **The visual harness's unit page now carries its narration**, so the transport's width and
  its controls are something a browser reading can judge at all.
