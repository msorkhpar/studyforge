## What landed

- ⛔ **The block vocabulary changed, and it is said first.** A list item is a **string** when it holds no nested list, and otherwise an **array of its parts in reading order**: text runs and whole `list` blocks, whose items follow the same rule. Stated in [spec §6](../../specs/2026-09-08-studyforge-v1-design.md#6-the-ingestion-contract) and in `archive.blocks.item_parts`, which every consumer reads an item through.
- `archive.markdown.listing` reads a marker indented under an item, at any indent and after a blank line or none, as that item's nested list. Two new patterns hold the any-indent markers, `NESTED_UNORDERED` and `NESTED_ORDERED`, and they are asked only inside a list.
- `render.page.blocks.prose._listing` renders a nested list inside its parent `<li>`. `narrate.speakable.script._items` speaks it inside the parent item's clip, numbering its items when that list is ordered.

## Decisions

- **Read, not refuse.** Refusing would refuse `src/2.md` and block `ISO-09`. The corpus would then have to re-indent its own source (which R19 allows), but that fixes a reader's defect by editing the author's material.
- **Parts, not `{"text", "list"}`.** Measured over every markdown file at the three pinned corpora: `Claude-senior-java-engineer`'s `01-java-basics/README_1.1.1.md` continues an item with a paragraph after its nested list, then opens a second one. A pair would have to refuse a file that reads today, or move the paragraph ahead of its list.
- **Not a `raw_api` change.** `list` keeps its three fields and count key, and a document with no nested list is byte-identical. Over the three corpora, the files with no nested list read identically. The rest differ only inside list blocks, and their words match exactly.
- **Narration stays one clip per top-level item.** The speech-id grammar is unchanged, and nested `<li>`s carry no audio attribute. The highlight sits on the parent item, which holds the nested list.
- **`walk` and `counts_of` do not see a nested list.** It is part of its item, not a block in reading order. `assert_clean` and `content_sha256` walk every value, so the gate and the digest cover it.
- Consumers checked: `git grep` over `src` names two readers of `items`, `prose._listing` and `script._items`, and both changed. `archive.scrub`, `archive.document.content_sha256` and `tests/fixture_checks.strings_in` walk values generically and are unchanged.
