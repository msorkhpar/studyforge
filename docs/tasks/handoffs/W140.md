## Decisions

- ⛔ **THE FINDING IS THE INBOUND LINK AND THE HEADING IS A NOTICE** — clause 2, taken
  literally. ⚠️ **Measured consequence, and it is the reason the clause is right: 22 of 22
  collisions currently have no inbound link, and 20 of those 22 are in documents NO OFFICE
  MAY EDIT** (the archive and five ruling records — Ruling 106). ⭐ **A check that fired on
  the heading would open red on 22 findings nobody is permitted to fix, which is a check
  somebody switches off — `pointers.py`'s own founding argument, arriving from the other
  end.**
- ⛔ **AN EXPLICIT SUFFIX IS NOT A FINDING.** ⭐ `#what-settles-it-1` addresses the second
  heading deterministically under the same rule `heading_slugs` and GitHub both apply; only
  the BASE name is ambiguous. ⚠️ **Measured at my ref: the tree contains ZERO links of
  either shape, so this decision costs nothing today and is asserted by a test rather than
  by the tree.**
- ⛔ **THE CENSUS PRINTS ONE LINE PER DOCUMENT, NOT ONE PER NAME.** ⭐ Ruling 128 is
  discharged — every colliding name is printed with its count, and nothing is summed into a
  scalar that conceals it — ⚠️ **but the document is the unit of REMEDY (a minting office is
  asked to make the heading it is writing unique in the document it is writing it in), and
  grouping there makes the notice grow with DOCUMENTS rather than with NAMES: the archive
  can take a thousand more duplicate headings without the floor gaining a line.**
- ⛔ **A SIBLING MODULE RATHER THAN A SECTION OF `pointers.py`.** ⚠️ **MEASURED: `pointers.py`
  is `379` of R11's `400` at my tip after `+33` lines; the census, its two dataclasses and
  its notice are `234` more.** ⭐ **Ruling 261 — a ceiling is not a budget — and the seam is
  not invented for the occasion: the two halves answer different questions, one *what does
  this document answer to*, the other *how many things answer to this name*.**
- ⛔ **ORDERED BY INBOUND POINTERS, NOT BY DEPTH** — clause 2's word, and it is the order of
  COST. ⚠️ **Today it is a no-op because nothing has an inbound link; it is asserted by a
  test that plants a shallow collision with a link and a deep one without, and demands the
  shallow one first.**
