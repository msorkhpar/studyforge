## Decisions

### ⛔ The page chrome — ownership ruled, and it does **not** block M1

⭐ **Ownership follows the CTO's: the markup is `SF-12`'s and is DONE; the rules
belong with `reading.css`, because a class name with no rule is not styling.**
⛔ **So it is a task against `render/assets/` + `render/pageassets/`, minted as
`SF-34`, and it is NOT a re-opening of `SF-12`.**

⛔ **On the blocking half I ruled against the CTO**, and the reason is the
decomposition's own second half rather than taste:

- ⛔ **M1 explicitly does NOT require cross-unit navigation or a contents page**,
  and the outline and the between-units bar **are** that chrome. ⚠️ **Styling them
  at M1 means writing rules for regions whose targets M1 refuses to build.**
- ⭐ **The masthead is the only region M1's single page populates, and it is
  legible unstyled.** ⛔ **Rows 4 and 5 are what *"with styles"* decomposes into —
  references RESOLVE, highlighting is BOUNDED — and the chrome touches neither.**
- ⭐ **`SF-12` addressed all three regions by element / `aria-label` / `data-*`
  only**, measured against both goldens, ⛔ **so the rules can arrive later with no
  page change and no re-render.** ⚠️ **A bounded late cost is the condition under
  which a PO defers rather than blocks; an unbounded one is not.**
- ⛔ **`Q18` and Ruling 72 bind me**: a tenth row at close, for regions M1 declines
  to populate, converts a reachable finish line into a receding one.

⛔ **I did not wait for `QA-03`'s screenshot and I did not defer silently.** ⭐ **Row
8 gains one clause instead: the screenshot must RECORD the chrome's unstyled
appearance as a named observation** — ⛔ **and the trigger is stated so the ruling
is reversible on evidence: if the unstyled chrome makes the reading column
unreadable, row 8 FAILS, `SF-34` is pulled into M1.** ⚠️ **Legibility is the bar,
not polish, and naming the bar in advance is what stops row 8 becoming taste.**

### ⛔ `SF-12/4` — `exercises: false` is a **declaration of zero**, and it routes to `SF-28`

⭐ **One-directional on purpose:** a manifest carrying `"exercises": false`
declares zero, and every unit document built from that corpus carries
`practices.declared = 0`; ⛔ **`exercises: true` implies nothing for any unit.**
⚠️ **That keeps it a translation of a stated fact rather than a guess, and R1-clean.**

⛔ **No new task, and that is cheaper rather than lazier.** `build()` already
takes `declared_practices`; ⭐ **the only thing missing is a caller, and the first
caller that ever exists is `SF-28` (M4 step 4.3)** — the same milestone at which
the *complete at M4, not short* promise first becomes testable. ⛔ **M4 is the
earliest ref at which the defect can be OBSERVED, so the clause is landed on the
task that will be holding the instrument.**
