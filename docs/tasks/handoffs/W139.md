## Decisions

1. ⛔ **TWO LINES rather than one longer line, and it is clause 2 made structural.** ⭐ A
   reader cannot accidentally add two figures that are printed as two readings with two
   denominators, and the mirror asserts the property directly: a landing BELOW the window
   moves the second line and leaves the first **byte-identical**, and a landing INSIDE it does
   the reverse. ⚠️ **Byte-identity is the strongest available statement of *never summed*** —
   no merged scalar could survive it.
2. ⭐ **NAMED, and its cardinality printed BESIDE the names with its denominator.** ⛔ Clause 1
   forbids a *second scalar* standing in for the set, not a count standing beside it — and
   Ruling 48 forbids a count with no denominator. ⚠️ At my ref that line names **109** ids and
   is long; ⭐ the row's own argument is why it is not shortened — *a bare count is the form
   that produced this defect*.
3. ⭐ **The below-window subject is the DERIVED SERIES, never the citation set.** ⛔ So a number
   outside the contiguous series can never enter it however it is spelled in a convention —
   which is what the REFUSE control measures, and why `9999` in somebody's probe table cannot
   become a member.
4. ⭐ **A third EMPTY joins Ruling 191's two.** ⛔ A tree whose whole series fits inside the
   window has nothing below it, and the notice SAYS SO — *"the 25-newest window covers the
   whole derived series (3 ruling(s))"* — rather than printing `0 of 0`, which is Ruling 48's
   defect written by the instrument that exists to avoid it.
5. ⭐ **The split was cut along the QUESTION, not at line 400.** `citations.py` answers *what
   does this SPELLING mean*; `reach.py` answers *did this RULING land*. ⛔ Three of the four
   declared gaps are the grammar's and moved with it; **the fourth — a CITATION is not a
   LANDING — is a property of what `reach.py` concludes and stayed there.** ⚠️ Ruling 258's
   closed claim is therefore now closed in two documents, and each half asserts its own.
6. ⛔ **`MAX_RANGE_SPAN` could not stay an assignment**, because the seam runs one way and
   `citations` may not import `reach`. ⭐ So it is `25` in `citations.py` with its reason, and
   `MAX_RANGE_SPAN == REACH_WINDOW` is ASSERTED in `test_reach.py` — a red test rather than a
   silent divergence, which is what the equality was worth all along.
7. ⭐ **The live both-directions plant ran on a COPY of the documents, not on the tree.**
   ⛔ Ruling 287 and `W128/3` describe how to plant and restore safely on the host; ⭐ a copy
   pays none of that cost and proves it by printing `git status --porcelain` empty at both ends
   of the invocation. ⚠️ It is the same population — the eight live convention documents and
   the 49 live ruling records, copied byte for byte.
