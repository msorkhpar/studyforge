## Decisions

⭐ **Which merge closes a wave: both offices' rounds, in PRECEDENCE, `cto` then `po`, each round counted ONCE.** A `cto` round closes a wave until `GROWTH_WAVES` distinct `po` rounds have merged after the newest `cto` round. From then on `po` rounds close waves. A round branch is still `OFFICE` from [`unclaimed.py`](../../../tools/quality/board/unclaimed.py), matched WHOLE ([`W245`](W245.md), [the convention](../../conventions/delivery-flow.md#office-round-branches)), so a topic branch under the round prefix is still no close.

The three candidates, measured at `fb89041`, host, `git log --first-parent --merges` read with this branch's `merged_branch` and `OFFICE`:

| candidate | reads the history BEFORE (while `cto` rounds merged) | reads it AFTER (no CTO reviewer) |
|---|---|---|
| `cto` only (as shipped) | as recorded | ⛔ frozen at `2912a33`, the witness `W245/1` |
| `po` only | ⛔ its base drifts from the `cto` base at almost every `cto` close. At `7a7a178` it classes 2 modules near and moving, where the docstring's frozen reading is 0 | moves |
| a named wave-close marker | ⛔ no marker exists. Only three first-parent subjects (PO rounds 64 to 66) say a wave closed, and a marker would be a new convention outside this surface | ⛔ no window at all |
| ⭐ **precedence, `cto` then `po`** | ⭐ **identical to `cto` only at every one of the 254 first-parent merge refs up to and including `2912a33`: 0 differ.** No `cto`-era ref had more than 2 distinct `po` rounds after its newest `cto` round | ⭐ moves off `cto` at `chore/po-round60` (`8f2f4b3`). - ⭐ **A window never mixes the two populations**, so no frozen record changes its meaning (Ruling 106). Every reading this module took, at `7a7a178`, `428223c` and `2fe2afb`'s `ad74a55`, is a `cto`-era window and reads the same. No record is edited.
- ⭐ **Once per ROUND, not per merge (`W245/2`).** `chore/po-round58` and `chore/po-round17` merged twice, and several `cto` rounds merged two to four times. Measured: this moves the `cto` window at 53 refs, the newest of them `chore/cto-round48`, older than [`W155`](../BOARD-ARCHIVE.md#w155-r11s-ceiling-has-an-instrument-and-its-approach-has-none-the-predicate-is-proximity-growth-and-proximity-alone-flags-the-ceiling-working)'s founding window at `428223c`. No reading this instrument took moves.
- ⭐ **Clause 2: the window line always names the close its waves END at.** When a round of another office has merged after that close, it prints *"NOT the last 3 waves"* and names those rounds, never *"the last 3 waves"*. It stays a notice, and no exit moves.
- Reversible: one tuple, and the precedence in one function.
