## Decisions

- ⛔ **Ruled: the root is `archive/`, not `.studyforge/archive/`.** Reversible, and recorded here.
  - R2 makes `validate` the adapter's definition of done. It read `archive/`, and so did the
    published adapter skill ([`SKILL.md`](../../../src/studyforge/skills/adapter/SKILL.md) states
    `<corpus-root>/archive/`), `Layout` (which refuses a leading dot), the scaffold, a build and
    every fixture.
  - Spec §6 leaves `<archive-root>` unnamed. Only §5's worked example and `plan`'s one composition
    said `.studyforge/archive/`, and nothing read that root.
  - Choosing `.studyforge/archive/` would move SK-02's published layout and every adapter it has
    generated. The brief routes that as a finding (1 and 2 below), not a silent migration.
- **`no-archive` is a `Finding`.** `report.py` keeps `Unchecked` for "absence is itself a validated
  fact" and says an unchecked claim "does not make it false". Bending that vocabulary so an
  `Unchecked` moves the exit would break both. The archive is the subject of the verdict, so its
  absence is a finding.
- **Declared as an office handoff, scope `INT06-5-6`.** The floor's task-ID pattern refuses
  `INT06-5-6`. Declaring `W241` would require a filename beginning `W241`, and the coordinator
  fixed this filename. It owes the same title, six sections and markers. The register reconciles
  it with `W241`.
- "Empty" means no `container.json` anywhere beneath the root. A map that is present but refused
  is already a `container` finding.
