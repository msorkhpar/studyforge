## Decisions

### 1. ⛔ `scrub` then `assert_clean` — and they read different words

The epic says *"every segment passes `scrub` then `assert_clean` before the
request leaves — the same order the archive writer uses"*. ⚠️ **Read as two
verbs applied to one string, that order is a gate that cannot fire**: `scrub`
replaces a home path with `/path/to/project`, and `assert_clean` then passes.
Three of the four shapes would be silently narrated aloud as placeholders.

⭐ **The archive writer's order is a division of SUBJECT, not a pipeline over one
string** (SF-08 *Decisions* 1, whose sentence is *"scrub our words; refuse the
source's"*). So here:

- `scrub` reads **what this framework wrote** — the `where` label, and the
  sentence `ServiceUnavailable` carries;
- `assert_clean` reads **what a source wrote** — the segment text, the ids, the
  voice, the format and the URL, all of them refused and none rewritten.

⛔ **Measured, and it is the load-bearing consequence**: a clean segment reaches
the wire byte-for-byte, asserted by decoding the recorded body and comparing it
to `unit.speak`. A client that scrubbed segments would pass every "does it
raise" test and fail this one.

### 2. ⭐ The gate is at the seam as well as per segment, and the seam is the property

⛔ **A gate that lived only in `narrate()` would be a discipline.** `_send`
builds the URL, runs `assert_clean` over `{"url": …, "payload": …}` **decoded**,
and only then encodes the body — so the object that is gated is the object that
would be serialised, and there is no path through the module on which bytes
reach a transport unpassed. An AST test asserts there is **exactly one** call
site handing work to a transport and that it lives in `_send`.

⚠️ The per-segment layer is kept because it is the **actionable** one: its
`where` names the speech id, and the seam's cannot.

### 3. ⛔ Ruling 58 — the refusal travels through, and it is asserted

`PersonalDataLeak` is outside `NarrationError` and is never translated into it.
⭐ **Asserted two ways**: `not issubclass(PersonalDataLeak, NarrationError)`, and
an AST scan of this module's every `except` arm that refuses a bare `except`,
`Exception`, `BaseException`, and any arm naming the leak. `SpeakableError`
travels through unconverted too, and the module contract names both — which is
the rubric's own remedy: *a contract that names what crosses it is a better
contract than one that swallows what crosses it*.

### 4. ⭐ The service's own `url` is not followed

A manifest entry carries `url`, and a client that followed it would let a third
party's answer choose where the next request goes. ⛔ The path is rebuilt from
`artifact_id` against the configured base, after the id is matched against
`\A[0-9a-f]{16,128}\Z` and the format against `\A[a-z0-9]{1,8}\Z` — so neither
can become a path segment or a filename suffix of the service's choosing.
Measured with `../../etc/passwd` and three other shapes.

### 5. ⛔ `place` takes its directory; it never asks for a profile

The client could have imported `corpus.placement` and asked the profile itself.
⚠️ That would have made it a second reader of the layout policy — correct today
and a second authority forever. ⭐ The caller asks and passes the answer in, and
`place` has no default, which is why *"it decides nothing about where they land"*
is a signature property rather than a promise.

### 6. Atomic placement, carried from `NS-01`

Each clip lands in a temporary dot-prefixed sibling and is renamed over its
target, so an interrupted run leaves no truncated file that looks finished.

for one commit, and nothing in the green run said so.

⭐ **Not the gate's defect** — reading tracked files is the right subject for a

## For dependents

**`SF-17` (the synthesis pass) — you are the caller this was built for.**

- ⛔ **Call `narrate(units)` once per batch, not once per unit.** The client
  submits one job; per-request overhead is the difference the epic names.
  ⚠️ **The 4 MiB body cap is the service's and this client does not split for
  you** — chunk a large corpus into several `narrate()` calls, and overlap costs
  nothing because the cache is content-addressed.
- ⛔ **Read `Narration.failed` and `Narration.retryable`, never a status code.**
  A well-formed job answers `200` whatever its segments did. `retryable` means
  *the job stopped early: resubmit the identical job and the finished segments
  are free*; `failed` means *your input is wrong*.
- ⭐ **`place(narration, into)` and `into` is yours to obtain**, from
  `corpus.placement`'s profile for the corpus:
  `profile_for(manifest.placement).unit(address, ordinal, title, origin=…).media_dir(AUDIO_DIRNAME)`,
  joined to the corpus root. ⛔ **Do not pass a directory you composed** — that
  is the second layout authority R4 removes, and `NS-05/2` measures why the
  mistake is invisible under one profile.
- ⛔ **Nothing is written until you call `place`.** That is what makes *"no
  partial state"* true, and it is also a constraint: a job's audio is in memory
  until then, so size your batches for that as well as for the body cap.
- ⭐ **`probe()` before a build, and treat `reachable=False` as a decision
  point** — a corpus with no narration reads fine (R6, R8). `Health.detail` is
  already scrubbed and is safe to print.
- ⚠️ **`Artifact.engine` and `engine_model` are what made THAT file**, and on a
  cache hit they are the first synthesis's, which may not be what is deployed.
  Record them at the moment you place, not at the moment you build.
- ⛔ **Reconciliation is not here.** Nothing is garbage-collected at either end:
  an edited unit's old clip stays on disk under its old name, and the service
  keeps its old artifact under its old address.
- ⚠️ **Incremental regeneration is not here either** — see `NS-05/8`. This client
  submits everything and lets the cache decide. If you want to decide before
  submitting, the recipe is in the service's `consuming.json` under `api.cache`
  and the budget is on `Health.chunk_chars`.

**`E04`'s renderer.** A placed clip is `<speech id>-<8 hex>.<format>`, minted by
`speakable.naming.clip_name` from the whole `SpeechUnit`. ⛔ **Ask
`UnitLocations.href(AUDIO_DIRNAME, filename)` for the page's link** rather than
composing it — the string differs by profile and only the "relative to the page"
invariant survives both.

**Anyone editing `narrate/client.py` next.**

- ⛔ **It is at `398/400`. Split at the seam `NS-05/5` names; do not trim.**
  Trimming this file means deleting the argument for why the gate is where it is.
- ⛔ **Do not add a second call site that hands work to a transport.** An AST
  test asserts there is exactly one and that it lives in `_send`; that single
  seam is what makes the acceptance a property instead of a habit.
- ⛔ **Do not wrap a leak.** No arm may catch `PersonalDataLeak`, and an AST test
  refuses a bare `except`, `Exception` and `BaseException` too, because those
  catch it by accident (Ruling 58).
- ⚠️ **If you change what `place` writes, read `NS-05/2` first.** Half the R4
  test cannot fail for a whole class of defect, by a property of the `tree`
  profile rather than by a weakness in the test.
- ⭐ **The import set is asserted exactly.** Adding a dependency turns
  `test_the_module_imports_only_the_standard_library_and_two_framework_modules`
  red on purpose — answer the question in a handoff rather than editing the set.
