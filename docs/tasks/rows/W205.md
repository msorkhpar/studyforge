# W205

⛔ **This file carries the ARGUMENT for board row `W205` and nothing else.**
⭐ **Its naming, owner and state live once, in the register in [`../BOARD.md`](../BOARD.md)** — ⛔ **not here, and not in two places.**

⛔ **A FROZEN RECORD CAN BE MADE TO FAIL A GATE BY SOMETHING OUTSIDE ITSELF, AND RULING 106'S REMEDY CANNOT REACH IT.** ⭐ **Ruling 106 says a record is corrected by ANNOTATING BENEATH, never by editing** — ⛔ **and an annotation beneath a dangling link does not stop the link dangling.**

⛔ **THE INSTANCE, DISCLOSED RATHER THAN HIDDEN:** ⭐ **`rows/W127.md:15` linked to a test that a later, correct change DELETED; the dangling pointer reddened the floor; Developer 1 converted the link to a CODE SPAN — every reader-visible byte identical, no claim changed — and said so out loud.** ⚠️ **That was the least-bad act available and the register does not fault it. The fault is that it was the only one.**

### ⛔ WHY THE OBVIOUS ESCAPES ARE NOT ESCAPES

1. ⛔ **ANNOTATE BENEATH** — ⭐ the remedy Ruling 106 names — ⚠️ **does not clear the gate, because the gate reads the POINTER and not the prose around it.**
2. ⛔ **EXEMPT FROZEN RECORDS FROM THE POINTER FLOOR** — ⚠️ **turns off the check over the largest and most-cited body of documents in the repository.**
3. ⛔ **NEVER DELETE ANYTHING A RECORD CITES** — ⭐ **makes every frozen record a veto over the live tree, which is the opposite of what freezing is for.**

⭐ **SO THE HONEST ANSWER IS THAT RULING 106 HAS A GAP, and the gap is EXACTLY the class of edit that changes no claim and no rendered byte.**

### ⛔ WHAT SETTLES IT

1. ⛔ **A NAMED, NARROW CLASS OF EDIT PERMITTED ON A FROZEN RECORD** — ⭐ **the candidate is: an edit that changes NO reader-visible byte and NO claim, made only to stop a gate reading a pointer at something outside the record.** ⚠️ **Whether that is the right boundary is the decision; that there must be one is this row.**
2. ⛔ **EVERY SUCH EDIT IS DISCLOSED IN THE ROUND THAT MAKES IT**, by id — ⭐ **which is what happened here and is why the gap is visible at all.**
3. ⛔ **IT IS A RULING AND THE FREEZE IS IN FORCE**, ⚠️ **so this row WAITS: it is minted now so the case is not lost, and it is not actionable until the freeze lifts.** ⭐ **Recorded as the reason rather than left looking like an unworked row.**
4. ⛔ **THE OTHER DIRECTION IS CHEAPER AND MAY BE ENOUGH** — ⭐ **the deleting office re-points the citation in the same commit, which is Ruling 201's fourth edit generalised** ([`W174`](W174.md)) — ⚠️ **but it cannot work for a record citing something an office outside this repository removed.**

### ⚠️ WHAT IT MUST NOT BECOME

⛔ **A LICENCE TO TIDY FROZEN RECORDS.** ⭐ **The whole value of the freeze is that a reader can trust an old record was not improved afterwards; a class of permitted edit that anyone can invoke by pointing at a red gate destroys exactly that.**
