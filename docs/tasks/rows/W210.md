⛔ **This file carries the ARGUMENT for board row `W210` and nothing else.**
⭐ **Its naming, owner and state live once, in the register in [`../BOARD.md`](../BOARD.md)** — ⛔ **not here, and not in two places.**

⛔ **THE MEDIA POLICY IS SPELLED TWICE.** ⭐ **`cli/plan/report.py`'s `MediaProjection.ignored` returns `not policy.commits`, which is exactly what `media.ignore_lines` exists to be the ONE spelling of.** ⚠️ **Raised by Developer 1 as `SF-32/4`, against `SF-31`'s surface, as a finding rather than a patch.**

### ⛔ BOTH ARE CORRECT TODAY, AND THAT IS THE ENTIRE POINT

⭐ **Nothing is broken and nothing will be until the policy grows.** ⛔ **`MediaPolicy.commit` is a CLOSED SET of three, and the one property that reads it — `commits` — already carries a docstring explaining why `auto` answers yes, because that answer is the counter-intuitive one.** ⚠️ **A second site that re-derives the same answer from the same property is one edit away from disagreeing with the first**, and the disagreement would be a plan that reports a corpus's media as ignored while the build commits it.

⭐ **[`W60`](W60.md)'s shape — two copies of one vocabulary — and [`W13`](W13.md)'s, where the two copies of one gate ALREADY disagreed by the time anyone looked.**

### ⛔ WHAT SETTLES IT

1. ⛔ **THERE IS ONE SPELLING AND THE OTHER SITE CALLS IT** — ⭐ **`ignore_lines` is the named home, so the plan's projection asks it rather than re-deriving from `commits`.**
2. ⚠️ **IF THE TWO ARE NOT ACTUALLY THE SAME QUESTION, THAT IS THE FINDING AND IT IS BIGGER** — ⛔ **`ignore_lines` answers *what does the ignore file say*, and `MediaProjection.ignored` answers *is this corpus's media ignored*, and if a case exists where those differ the row's job is to name it, not to collapse them.** ⭐ **The taker checks that before deleting either.**
3. ⛔ **ASSERTED, NOT INSPECTED** (R12): ⭐ **one test over all three commit modes that reads both sites and requires they agree** — ⚠️ **which is the assertion that would have failed on the day they diverged, and which nothing holds today.**
4. ⚠️ **`cli/plan/` IS `SF-31`'s SURFACE AND `corpus/manifest/media.py` IS NOT THIS ROW'S** — ⛔ **the fix belongs on the CLI side, calling outward.**

### ⚠️ WHAT IT MUST NOT BECOME

⛔ **A THIRD HELPER THAT BOTH SITES CALL.** ⭐ **`ignore_lines` already is that helper** — ⚠️ **and adding a fourth spelling to unify two is how [`W133`](W133.md)'s standing split condition came about.**
