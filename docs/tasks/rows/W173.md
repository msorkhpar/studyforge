### ⛔ WHY IT IS NOT ONE MORE PO TIDY-UP

⭐ **The board is the one document every agent opens as the CURRENT state**, and a rule written there is read at session start as a snapshot — ⛔ **which is `CLAUDE.md`'s own argument, applied to the file `CLAUDE.md` sends every reader to.** ⚠️ **A rule cannot be kept true in a snapshot any more than a fact can; it can only be kept freshly wrong.**

⛔ **AND IT HAS ALREADY COST A ROUND.** ⚠️ **PO round 56 found a Standing decisions cell asserting *"a slot the open step cannot fill takes the queue head"* against a record that says only that EMPTINESS is the finding — a clause with no source, typed into the board from the previous round's own handoff, and binding enough that reading it literally would have charged the coordinator with a defect they had not committed** (`PO-56/2`).

### ⛔ WHAT SETTLES IT

1. ⛔ **A PREDICATE THAT TELLS A POINTER FROM A RESTATEMENT**, and it is the hard part — ⚠️ **`board-frame`'s own history is the warning: `startswith` and `in` cannot read a contradiction, and widening an instrument to judge whether prose ARGUES rebuilds the gate Ruling 180 removed.** ⭐ **A closed predicate is preferred to a threshold** (Ruling 186's remedy): a citation with no imperative beside it is an address; a citation attached to a sentence that states the rule is a copy.
2. ⛔ **THE FIVE BOARD-ONLY RULINGS LAND IN A CONVENTION FIRST, AND THE ROW DOES NOT REMOVE THEM UNTIL THEY HAVE** — ⭐ **removing a rule no convention carries is not applying Ruling 349, it is losing the rule.** ⚠️ **That half is `docs/conventions/`'s surface and is ordered before the board half.**
3. ⚠️ **THE SWEEP IS INCREMENTAL AND EACH REMOVAL NAMES ITS DESTINATION** — ⛔ **the removing round records, per paragraph, the convention document and the string that carries the rule**, which is the form PO round 56 used for its own three removals and which makes each one checkable after the fact.
4. ⛔ **ASSERTED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS** (R12): a board paragraph that RESTATES a carried rule is named, and a board paragraph that merely ADDRESSES one is silent — ⭐ **and the silent arm is the one worth planting, because a notice that flags correct pointers is one the register learns to scroll past** (Ruling 179).
5. ⚠️ **A CEILING IS NOT A BUDGET** (Ruling 261). ⛔ **This row must not be argued from the board's byte headroom.** ⭐ **The bytes are a symptom and they are not the defect** — a board with room to spare and a rule in the wrong home is exactly as wrong — ⚠️ **and pricing it in bytes is how `board-size` gets raised instead of obeyed.**

### ⚠️ WHAT IT MUST NOT BECOME

⛔ **A BOARD WITH NO PROSE.** ⭐ **A register legitimately says what it is, where its parts live and which table is which** — ⚠️ **the delimiters' own preamble is a pointer, not a rule, and stripping it would make the board unreadable to satisfy a count.** ⛔ **AND NOT A SECOND BYTE BOUND**: `board-narrative` already exists, it is not the constraint that bit, and a second ceiling below the first makes the real one unreachable.
