# W146

⛔ **This file carries the ARGUMENT for board row `W146` and nothing else.**
⭐ **Its naming, owner and state live once, in the register in [`../BOARD.md`](../BOARD.md)** — ⛔ **not in two places.**

⛔ **`PO-50/12`. RULING 246 PUT A TIP INTO THE COMMITS-AHEAD CELL SO THE READING WOULD CARRY ITS OWN AS-OF, AND THE ONE INSTRUMENT THAT READS THAT CELL PARSES THE NUMBER AND THROWS THE TIP AWAY.** ⭐ **So a cell that was TRUE WHEN IT WAS WRITTEN and a cell that was WRONG WHEN IT WAS WRITTEN print the same sentence, and the cell itself carries everything needed to tell them apart.**

### ⛔ THE MECHANISM, IN THE SHIPPED CODE

⭐ **`observation.Observation.commits` is `_COUNT.search(...)` over the `Commits ahead` cell and returns the FIRST integer it finds.** ⛔ **`verdict._cell()` compares that integer against `graph.ahead(branch)` — today's count — and nothing anywhere resolves the sha written beside it.** ⚠️ **The cell's declared form is Ruling 246's `n @ <branch tip>`, and the `@ <branch tip>` half reaches no reader at all.**

### ⭐ THE READING — MEASURED BY THIS OFFICE AT `504bb47`, role `wt/po`, THREE TAKES OF ONE PAIR OF CELLS IN ONE ROUND

⛔ **POPULATION PRINTED IN FULL BEFORE ANY SCALAR** (Rulings 123/128/140). ⚠️ **Every one of the three is case (b) — a reading true at its own declared ref, printed as a disagreement:**

```text
role wt/po, 504bb47 — the cells this register round wrote, and git's answer at each declared tip

take 1  W142+W143  0 @ 94ad941     W140  0 @ 94ad941      written at the In-flight re-take
take 2  W142+W143  0 @ 94ad941     W140  1 @ a8f0c92      W140's taker committed mid-round
take 3  W142+W143  1 @ a04e590     W140  1 @ a8f0c92      W142/W143's taker committed mid-round

git, asked about the DECLARED tip rather than the branch:
  release/m0-foundations..94ad941   =  0     ⭐ the cell's own number, exactly
  release/m0-foundations..a8f0c92   =  1     ⭐ the cell's own number, exactly
  release/m0-foundations..a04e590   =  1     ⭐ the cell's own number, exactly
⛔ 3 of 3 cells TRUE AT THEIR OWN DECLARED REF, and the shipped notice printed a disagreement
   for each of them the moment the branch moved past it.
```

⛔ **SO THE NOTICE CANNOT SAY WHICH OF TWO THINGS HAPPENED, AND THEY ARE NOT THE SAME THING:** ⭐ **(a) the cell was WRONG when it was written — the office miscounted — and (b) the cell was RIGHT when it was written and the branch moved.** ⚠️ **(a) is a defect in the register; (b) is the register behaving exactly as Ruling 97 and Ruling 246 ask it to.**

### ⚠️ THIS IS NOT A REVERSAL OF `PO-42/7` AND MUST NOT BECOME ONE

⛔ **`PO-42/7` ruled that a count disagreement is a NOTICE and NEVER a refutation, on a measured ground: refuting would fire on every wave in which somebody commits after the board is written, which is every wave.** ⭐ **THAT STANDS UNTOUCHED AND THIS ROW DOES NOT ASK FOR ONE BYTE OF IT.** ⚠️ **What this row asks is that the NOTICE SAY MORE, not that the VERDICT say something else** — ⛔ **and the distinction is exactly the middle path `PO-49/10` and `PO-50/11` recorded as earning its keep.**

### ⛔ WHAT SETTLES IT

1. ⭐ **The `Commits ahead` cell's DECLARED TIP is parsed and resolved, and the notice states which of the two cases it is** — ⛔ **`DATED` where `release..<declared tip>` equals the declared count, and a disagreement only where it does not.** ⚠️ **The word for case (b) is `DATED` and never `stale`, `refuted` or `wrong`** (Ruling 97, Ruling 310(b), and `CTO-62/2`, which is that mislabel ruled against the office that made it).
2. ⛔ **AN UNRESOLVABLE TIP IS RULING 216's THIRD ANSWER AND NOT A DISAGREEMENT.** ⭐ **A sha git cannot resolve — a cell written against a rewritten branch, or a typo — must not fall through to either of the two cases**; ⚠️ **`Graph.ahead()` already returns `None` for exactly this and `verdict` already checks it FIRST, so the new reading inherits that discipline rather than inventing a fourth arm.**
3. ⛔ **A CELL THAT DECLARES NO TIP IS UNCHANGED.** ⭐ **The `@ <tip>` half is Ruling 246's form and older cells predate it; a row that declares only a number keeps exactly the notice it has today** — ⚠️ **a remedy that turned a missing tip into a finding would fire on correct historical work, which is Ruling 179's cost and Ruling 185(a)'s unsatisfiable-on-day-one defect.**
4. ⛔ **ASSERTED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS** (R12): a cell true at its declared tip must read `DATED` and a cell FALSE at its own declared tip must still be reported as a disagreement — ⭐ **and the second half is the one that is easy to leave untested, because every instance this round measured was case (b).**
5. ⚠️ **R11, MEASURED at `504bb47`: `verdict.py` is `365` of `400` and `observation.py` `353`.** ⛔ **Neither is at the ceiling and neither is a budget** (Ruling 261) — ⭐ **but `tools/quality/board/` already carries a standing SPLIT condition on `register.py` at `399`, so a taker who finds themselves trimming rather than splitting is in the wrong module.**

### ⚠️ WHAT IT MUST NOT BECOME

⛔ **A SECOND PARSE OF THE CELL.** ⭐ **The number and the tip come out of ONE cell and belong to ONE reader** — ⚠️ **`observation.py` already owns `commits`, so a tip parsed in `verdict.py` would put two halves of one grammar in two modules, which is the seam `W139` paid to cut properly one package over.**

⛔ **AND NOT A COMPARISON THE ROW DID NOT MAKE.** ⚠️ **A cell naming SEVERAL branches is already PRINTED and not compared, because one cell is not a claim about N branches** — ⭐ **the declared tip does not change that and must not be used to invent a per-branch claim the row never wrote.**
