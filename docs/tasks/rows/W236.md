⭐ **It does NOT hold `SK-06` or `M4`:** the Acceptance says *progress*, which is the progress record, and `M4`'s sentence says *practice passes*. It jumps nobody.

### ⛔ WHAT SETTLES IT

1. ⛔ **A reader can carry their read marks into an owner archive and back.** The entry point is the taker's to argue, and the browser side is `SF-30`'s contract.
2. ⛔ **A sharing archive carries none**, asserted, as progress is today.
3. ⛔ **The state route still never treats a read mark as a pass** (`E05` § `SF-21`).
