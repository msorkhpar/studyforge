### ⛔ WHY IT CANNOT BE FIXED AFTER THE FACT

⛔ **Ruling 320: a landed merge subject is FROZEN and cannot be annotated.** ⭐ **So `19c7224`'s subject stays wrong forever, and the only available remedy is a gate that runs BEFORE the merge.** ⚠️ **That is precisely why a practice is insufficient here: the failure is unrecoverable rather than correctable.**

### ⛔ WHAT SETTLES IT

1. ⛔ **A subject that `tools.quality.subject` refuses CANNOT reach a release branch** — the check is in the merge path, not in an office's habit.
2. ⚠️ **WHERE the gate lives is the taker's call and must be argued out loud:** the floor cannot read a branch position (Ruling 80), and `corroborate`'s own docstring records why a floor check may not shell into git — so a naive *"add it to `CHECKS`"* is refused by an existing ruling. ⛔ **Read that argument before choosing.**
3. ⛔ **Asserted both ways (R12):** a subject carrying a bare count is refused by the gate, and a subject of properties passes.
4. ⚠️ **It must not punish the frozen past.** ⛔ **A report over landed subjects is a backlog no office may clear** (`subject.py`'s own *WHAT IT MUST NOT BECOME*), so the gate reads the subject being COMPOSED and never history.

### ⚠️ WHAT IT MUST NOT BECOME

⛔ **A CHECK THAT REWRITES OR PROPOSES A SUBJECT.** ⭐ It REFUSES and names the claim and the count; composing is the author's.
